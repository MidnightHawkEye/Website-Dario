Frequently Asked Questions

Can I have a PC built to my specifications?

Yes. I can help you plan a custom PC for your requirements, intended use and budget.
Whether it’s a gaming PC, office computer or powerful workstation, we’ll choose suitable components together. On request, I can also assemble it, set it up and carry out basic functionality checks.

Can I bring components I already own?

Yes. Existing hardware can be considered for a new build or an upgrade.
Before installation, I check whether your components are compatible with the system. Reusing suitable parts can help you avoid unnecessary costs.

Do you offer buying advice?

Yes. I can help you choose a new computer, suitable components or worthwhile upgrades.
I take your requirements, budget and desired performance into account. The aim is to find a solution that fits without unnecessary or overpriced components.

What does a PC clean involve?

First we discuss the condition of your computer and what you would like cleaned.
Dust is then carefully removed from the inside of the case and from accessible fans and cooling components. The service can also include a visual hardware inspection and system temperature checks.

Can I request a no-obligation quote?

Yes. Just tell me what you need using the contact form. I’m happy to review enquiries about custom builds, hardware advice, cleaning or maintenance.

I’ll prepare a tailored, no-obligation quote based on your requirements.

We discuss the cost and scope before any work is agreed. Additional work is carried out only by prior agreement.

What services does Blackridge Technical offer?

Blackridge Technical focuses on personal IT services for computer hardware.

Services include:

  • Custom PC planning and assembly
  • PC buying advice and hardware selection
  • PC interior cleaning and maintenance
  • Hardware upgrades
  • Windows installation and basic setup

The focus is on personal advice, careful work and clear costs.
