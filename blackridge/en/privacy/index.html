Hosting via GitHub Pages

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When a page is requested, the service receives the connection data needed to deliver it. This may include the IP address, requested page, time of access, browser and device information, and the referring page, to the extent your browser sends that information.

The data is used to operate and secure the website. GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages websites for security purposes. See the GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies, Tracking and External Resources

Blackridge Technical does not set its own cookies or use local storage or session storage. The website does not embed analytics, tracking or marketing services. Images, icons, stylesheets and scripts are served by the website. Fonts are chosen from those available on your device. External links take you to the relevant provider when opened.

Contact Form

If the contact form is available and you submit it, your browser sends your email address, selected subject, message and name, if provided, to Formspree, Inc. in the United States over HTTPS. The submission includes an empty technical field for spam prevention. Formspree may also process your IP address and other connection data, such as browser type, access time and the origin of the request.

Formspree stores submissions in the Blackridge Technical dashboard. If email notifications are configured, a copy may be sent to the configured recipient mailbox. Dario Hasler uses the information to process and respond to enquiries and feedback. Details of how the provider processes information are available in the Formspree Privacy Policy.

The required checkbox confirms only that you have read this privacy notice. The check takes place in your browser. The checkbox status is not sent with the form. The website does not store form entries in cookies or browser storage.

On the Free plan, Formspree says the dashboard displays submissions from the last 30 days. Formspree retains older submissions beyond this display limit. See Formspree account limits and the plan overview.

Contact via WhatsApp

WhatsApp opens through an external link when you select it. Blackridge does not load WhatsApp scripts or widgets. When you open the link, the external provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser or device data. The link on the contact page contains the Blackridge phone number and a pre-written message.

If you send a message, Dario Hasler receives its content and any data visible in the chat, such as your phone number and profile name where displayed. This information is used to process and respond to your enquiry. WhatsApp Ireland Limited provides the service for people in Switzerland. WhatsApp processes data under its own responsibility and may transfer it internationally. Details are available in the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

You choose whether to send a message and what to include. You can also contact me by email.

Contact by Email or Telephone

If you contact me by email, your sender address, the content and any attachments are used to process your enquiry. Google is involved in receiving messages through the Gmail address listed in the imprint. Further details are available in the Google Privacy Policy.

If you call, the information from the conversation that is needed to handle your enquiry is processed. The connection is established through the telephone providers involved. An email or telephone link passes the address or number to the corresponding app configured on your device.

Retention and Deletion of Personal Data

Enquiries are kept for as long as they are needed to handle the enquiry or for legitimate or legal retention purposes. Copies that are no longer needed must then be deleted. Depending on how you contacted us, this applies to the form dashboard entry, the email and, where applicable, the chat history.

Copies in the recipient mailbox and, where applicable, chat history or backups must also be taken into account. Data held by external services is also subject to their retention and deletion options. Formspree documents the deletion options in its dashboard, including for submissions over the account limit.

Disclosure of Personal Data Abroad

Personal data may be processed outside Switzerland as part of hosting and when using the contact methods described. The information below about countries and safeguards is drawn from the providers' public documentation.

  • GitHub: GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States. GitHub also identifies GitHub B.V. in the Netherlands and describes possible processing in the United States and other countries. For covered transfers, GitHub identifies the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and, depending on the transfer, standard contractual clauses. GitHub on international data transfers
  • Formspree: Formspree, Inc. identifies AWS in the United States as its hosting infrastructure. The provider states that processing may occur in other countries and identifies standard contractual clauses for its work as a processor. Formspree security information
  • WhatsApp: The regional provider is WhatsApp Ireland Limited in Ireland. Its policy names the United States, the United Kingdom and Singapore, among other countries, and refers to standard contractual clauses for certain transfers. WhatsApp LLC identifies the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Framework for data covered by its certification. WhatsApp Privacy Policy and Data Privacy Framework statement
  • Gmail: Google generally identifies Google Ireland Limited in Ireland as the provider for consumers in Switzerland. Google operates servers worldwide and identifies the Swiss–U.S. Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers to Google LLC in the United States. Google also identifies standard contractual clauses where required. Google on data transfers

Your Rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, you can ask whether and how your personal data is processed and request correction of inaccurate data. Where the legal conditions are met, you can request deletion or object to unlawful processing, for example by asking for it to stop or be restricted. In certain circumstances, you also have the right to receive or transfer your data.

Send your request by email or post to the controller listed above. Information on exercising your rights and contacting the supervisory authority is available from the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Data Security

The public website and submissions to Formspree use HTTPS. We need your email address to reply. Providing a name is optional. A hidden field helps block automated spam submissions.
