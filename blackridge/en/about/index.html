A Passion for Technology

I’m Dario Hasler, the founder of Blackridge Technical. I’m passionate about technology, computers and IT systems.

My background in industrial automation has given me a strong technical foundation and a structured approach to work.

Through Blackridge Technical, I want to put my enthusiasm for computer hardware and IT to practical use by helping individuals plan, build and maintain their PCs.

THE BLACKRIDGE APPROACH

BT / 001

Technology is more than hardware.

For me, good engineering starts with wanting to understand how something works and how it can be improved.

My background in industrial automation shapes how I work: analyse the system, implement the solution carefully and verify the result.

That’s the approach I want to bring to Blackridge Technical.

Analyse

Understand before you decide.

Identify requirements, understand how the technology fits together and find a suitable solution.

Implement

Work carefully and methodically.

Implement technical solutions with a clear plan and close attention to detail.

Verify

Check that it works and see what can be improved.

Check the results, identify potential weaknesses and make targeted improvements where needed.
